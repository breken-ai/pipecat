- `EvalJudge` decides every verdict with a `BaseClassifier`, which its constructor takes: an `LLMClassifier` over the LLM a `judge.eval:` block names, as before, or a `JevClassifier` for `service: typesafe`. The reasons behind the verdicts come from `EvalExplainer` (`pipecat.evals.explainer`), an LLM asked the same question, which the `explainer:` block configures and `explainer: false` turns off; it is asked for every `no` and every verdict below `explain_below`, and the classifier's verdict stands either way.
