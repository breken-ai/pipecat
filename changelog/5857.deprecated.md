- Passing an LLM service to `EvalJudge` (as its first argument or as `service=`), and its `max_tokens`, are deprecated and will be removed in 2.0.0. Build the judge with `EvalJudge(LLMClassifier(llm=service), explainer=EvalExplainer(service))`, which is what the service form does; `max_tokens` belongs to `EvalExplainer`.
