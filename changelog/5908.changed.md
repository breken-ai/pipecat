- ⚠️ **Behavior change:** Observers are now told about a frame once, on its first push, instead of on every push by every processor that passes it along. An observer that picks frames by the processor that pushed them, or logs every hop, needs every push. To keep the previous behavior, create the observer with `observe_every_push=True`; `FramePushed.first_push` then tells the first push from the ones that follow.
